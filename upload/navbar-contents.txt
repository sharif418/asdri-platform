3. Vision & Core Objectives

-   Vision Statement: Preparing thoughtful, knowledgeable, and effective
    dawah workers and researchers to address contemporary intellectual
    confusion and social fitnah, providing academic responses to
    misconceptions against Islam.

-   Core Pillars:

    -   Authentic Quran-Sunnah-based intellectual dawah.

    -   Bridging traditional Islamic sciences with modern disciplines
        (Psychology, Philosophy, Media, etc.).

    -   Character tarbiyah, leadership, and public engagement.

4. Featured Programs (Academics Preview)

Card grid featuring active key courses with quick details ([ Duration ],
[ Eligibility ], [ Learn More ]):

1.  Preparatory Year for Specialization (PYS): 3-Year residential
    flagship program for talented young Ulama focusing on higher dawah &
    modern challenges.

2.  Certificate Course in Islamic Studies (CCIS): 6-Month foundational
    course in Tajweed, basic Arabic, Fiqh, and Sirah for university
    graduates.

3.  Diploma in Dawah & Islamic Studies: 2-Year (4 Semesters) advanced
    diploma for CCIS graduates covering deep classical and modern dawah
    tools.

4.  Arabic Language Teacher Training: 15-Day intensive pedagogy and
    modern CLT-method training for Arabic scholars.

5.  Ramadan Dawah Training: 20-Day practical skill program covering
    contemporary issues, public speaking, and intellectual challenges.

6.  Azan Training Program: 15-Day course focusing on proper
    pronunciation (Makhraj), vocal technique, and Muazzin tarbiyah.
